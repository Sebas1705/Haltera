# Food database attribution

Generated by `scripts/nutrition/build.mjs`. Do not edit by hand.

## USDA FoodData Central

Nutrient values of the 705 bundled generic foods come from [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/), released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):

- Foundation Foods 2026-04-30
- SR Legacy 2018-04

U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central. fdc.nal.usda.gov.

## Open Food Facts

Barcode lookups use the [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org) database, available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0); its contents are under the Database Contents License (DbCL 1.0). Products found by barcode are cached on the device.

## Haltera

The selection of foods, their English and Spanish names and the serving sizes are by the Haltera authors.
