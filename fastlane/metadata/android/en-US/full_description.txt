A free, open source gym logbook. Log what you actually lift, on the days you actually train. No ads, no paywall, no tracking.

LOG YOUR WAY
• Routines of any cycle length: 3 days, 5 days, 6 on and 1 off, or any N-day cycle you like.
• Log any date and edit everything afterwards. Haltera follows your real training, it never forces a schedule.
• Equipment-aware logging: barbell, dumbbells, machines, cables, bodyweight, assisted and weighted movements, each with the right way to enter load.
• Automatic rest timers between sets.
• Personal records are flagged as you log them.

PROGRESS WITH A PLAN
• Double progression: set a rep range and a load step, and Haltera suggests when to go up.
• Progress charts per exercise and per muscle group, with a short explanation of what each number means.
• Routine analysis: a score and clear, optional suggestions about volume, balance and frequency, with references. It is advice only and never blocks or changes your routine.

EXERCISES AND ROUTINES
• A catalogue of 801 exercises covering strength, calisthenics, mobility and cardio, in English and Spanish.
• Copy well-known routines and make them yours, or build your own from scratch.
• Share routines as a file or QR code and import them on another phone.

NUTRITION LOG
• A database of 705 foods, with calories and macros.
• Log your meals, set daily goals and see how your days add up.

YOUR DATA
• Works offline. Your logbook is also backed up automatically to a private cloud space, so it survives a lost phone or a reinstall.
• Link a Google account if you want to keep that backup when you change phone or use several devices.
• Free and open source under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub.

Haltera is a logbook and a planning tool, not medical advice. Ask a professional before changing your training or diet if you have a health condition.
