Your training and nutrition logbook

Haltera is a free, open-source app to log what you actually lift and eat, on the days you actually train, and to see your progress. Android and iOS. No ads, no paywall, no analytics.

Source code on GitHub Privacy policy

What it does

  • Log your way

    Any routine cycle, any date, edit everything afterwards. It follows your real training and never forces a schedule.

  • Rest timers

    Automatic timers between sets, with an optional notification while you train.

  • Progression

    Rep ranges and load steps with double progression, personal records flagged as you log them.

  • Progress charts

    Per exercise and per muscle group, with a short explanation of each number.

  • Nutrition log

    Foods with calories and macros, barcode lookup through Open Food Facts, daily goals.

  • Your data

    Works offline. A cloud copy keeps your logbook safe when you change phone. Delete it any time.

Haltera is a logbook and planning tool, not medical advice. Ask a professional before changing your training or diet if you have a health condition.
